Sourcing qualification

What must be proved before supply.

  1. Confirm solution anneal, ferrite/phase balance and any required impact or corrosion testing.
  2. PREN is a chemistry index, not an approval certificate.
  3. Pressure/offshore projects may impose NORSOK, ISO 15156/NACE, DNV or customer-specific qualification requirements.
  4. Large plate width/thickness capability must be verified separately from grade capability.
