Three-source model

Resilience by design. Three legitimate supply routes.

Critical materials often need a primary manufacturing route, an approval/origin route and an urgent stock/processing route. The architecture is defined before a delay turns into an emergency.

A / Chinese MillCommercial-scale production, cost and non-standard development.
B / International OriginNamed-mill, origin-sensitive or early qualification requirements.
C / Stock & ProcessorSamples, small lots, urgent delivery and secondary processing.
Qualification gates

Capability claims are tested in stages.

We avoid equating a website grade list with an approved supply route.

  1. Gate 1 — Desk review: legal entity, product form, capacity, certificates and reference evidence.
  2. Gate 2 — Document challenge: MTC, heat number, label and source consistency.
  3. Gate 3 — Physical / process verification: plant, equipment, stock, outsourcing boundary and inspection.
  4. Gate 4 — Trial order: response, documentation, packaging, delivery and after-sales behaviour.
Route first

Choose the source around the constraint.

Named mill, urgent delivery, small lot, non-standard development and critical qualification should not use the same sourcing logic.
