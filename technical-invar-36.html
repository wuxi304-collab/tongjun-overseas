Sourcing qualification

What must be proved before supply.

  1. Do not assume 4J36, “Invar 36” and every K9360x UNS designation are automatically interchangeable.
  2. ASTM F1684 was withdrawn in 2024 with no replacement. ASTM B753 remains active but is scoped to thermostat component alloys; neither should be treated as a universal current procurement path for every 36Ni-Fe application.
  3. For LNG membrane use, confirm licensor/project approval, strip width/thickness, continuous length, weldability and batch consistency.
  4. For aerospace tooling, CTE is only one gate: residual stress, stress relief, UT, thermal cycling and flatness may control acceptance.
