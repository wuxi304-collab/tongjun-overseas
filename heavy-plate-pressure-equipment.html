Heavy fabrication

Extra-wide plate for pressure equipment.

Heavy-plate sourcing is dominated by usable width, thickness, heat treatment, internal quality and project documentation. A mill that can make the grade may still be unable to make the required plate geometry.

Application-specific sourcing
Plate geometry

Start with the actual finished plate requirement.

Width and thickness determine the realistic mill route, welding plan and fabrication yield.

01

Width

Extra-wide plate must be confirmed by grade and thickness—not from the rolling-mill nominal width alone.

02

Thickness

Heavy-gauge capability changes with alloy family, heat treatment and required flatness.

03

Single-plate weight

Plate length and weight can become logistics and heat-treatment constraints.

04

Flatness

Fabrication and vessel forming depend on realistic flatness after heat treatment.

05

Cut sizes

Project cut-to-size can reduce downstream handling and welding complexity.

06

Logistics

Export packing, lifting, port handling and container/breakbulk strategy are part of the route.

Qualification

Pressure-equipment plate requires an evidence package.

We distinguish base material availability from a project-qualified plate route.

01

UT

Internal quality class and ultrasonic test requirement are confirmed by standard/project.

02

PMI

Positive material identification can be included in the inspection plan.

03

3.1 / 3.2

Certificate and witness level must be specified before production.

04

Corrosion / impact

Where applicable, corrosion, impact or phase-balance requirements are added to the purchase specification.
