# RELEASE V20 — Corporate Role & Credibility Layer

Date: 2026-09-07

## Objective

V20 removes the remaining defensive/about-page language and makes responsibility boundaries explicit without inventing manufacturing ownership, legacy, approvals or facilities.

## About page

- Replaced “we are not a mill” with the positive operating position: **Our role is to qualify the route.**
- Added a three-layer Responsibility Architecture: Tongjun / Manufacturing Source / Buyer.
- Added a corporate identity ledger for brand, legal entity, operating base, supply model and technical contact.
- Removed the public-facing internal launch TODO.
- Reframed Wuxi as the operating base inside a major stainless-steel supply and processing hub.

## Control boundary

Tongjun owns route coordination and evidence planning. The manufacturing source owns manufacturing evidence. The buyer owns final approved-source, PO, inspection and project/OEM acceptance authority.

## Boundary

V20 deliberately avoids unsupported claims about owned mills, stock, certifications, approvals, global offices or manufacturing history. Corporate KYC details remain part of formal onboarding rather than marketing copy.
