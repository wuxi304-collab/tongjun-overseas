Source-role ledger

Four roles. Four different responsibilities.

Manufacture, custody, secondary processing and sourcing coordination are recorded as separate layers. That makes the origin story and the evidence path easier to audit.

01ManufacturerCreates the material

Heat / melt identity, manufacturing route, governing product specification and certified material data originate here.

02StockholderMaintains custody

Inventory identity, storage, cutting allocation and traceability back to the certified source remain visible.

03ProcessorChanges the form

Slitting, cut-to-length, machining, grinding or other secondary work adds a documented processing handoff without changing the declared manufacturing origin.

04Tongjun supply deskConnects the route

Requirement screening, source-role declaration, evidence planning, inspection coordination and backup-route design are assembled into one procurement path.

Evidence-to-offer boundary

The quotation inherits the evidence boundary.

A commercial offer should not silently widen a technical claim. Every price, lead-time or source route remains tied to the exact requirement and evidence assumptions used to screen it.

LOCKRequirement basis

Grade, product standard, geometry, condition, quantity and application are the quotation identity.

DECLAREEvidence basis

Core, conditional, PO-driven and buyer-controlled evidence are listed instead of implied.

TIME-BOUNDAvailability basis

Stock, mill slot, processing capacity and dispatch timing are reconfirmed at quotation / order release.

OPENUnresolved assumptions

Any unverified size, approval, origin, test or logistics item stays visible as an open condition.

Commercial disciplineA quote is not a certificate. A lead time is not capacity evidence. A low price is not an approval.Define the quotation basis →
Typical evidence

Documentation is part of the product.

  • Heat / lot identification
  • Mill Test Certificate (MTC)
  • EN 10204 3.1 / 3.2 support where applicable
  • PMI and chemistry verification
  • UT / NDT reports
  • Dimensional and surface inspection
  • Third-party inspection coordination
  • Export packaging and traceable labels
Qualification boundary

Capability and approval are separate gates.

A source may be technically capable of a grade while the buyer still requires a named mill, OEM approval, project AVL, specific melt route or additional qualification.

The sourcing proposal keeps those gates visible. Named-mill and approved-vendor requirements become route constraints rather than assumptions.
