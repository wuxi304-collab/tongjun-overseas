Route discipline

One RFQ can have three legitimate supply answers.

We keep manufacturing origin, stock origin and processing responsibility distinct so that a commercial shortcut does not become a qualification problem later.

A · China manufacturingBest for scalable, technically feasible requirements where Chinese origin is acceptable or can be qualified.
B · International-origin in ChinaBest where named origin or established mill approval matters but China-side stock and processing can improve delivery.
C · Stock & processingBest for trial quantity, urgent replacement, slitting, cut-to-length or a bridge before mill production.
