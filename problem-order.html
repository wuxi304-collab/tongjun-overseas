Problem Order Desk

Start with what is blocking the order.

A difficult special-metals order is usually blocked by one constraint: size, MOQ, lead time, origin, qualification, documentation or processing. Screen that constraint first. Then decide what must be proved before a quote is credible.

Use this for the awkward order. The result is a route hypothesis and evidence checklist. Stock, manufacturability and approval are then confirmed at the corresponding qualification gate.
Constraint-first sourcing
Keep confidential drawings, personal data and proprietary customer names out of this quick screen. Add them later through the agreed RFQ channel if needed.
Constraint screen

Complete the six route inputs.

The result will identify the likely sourcing route, the first evidence gate, the main commercial risk and the next technical action.

Decision discipline

Do not solve every problem with a cheaper mill.

A low quote can hide the real blocker. We separate manufacturability, buyer qualification, documentation, processing and delivery so that one commercial shortcut does not create a rejection later.

01 · FeasibilityCan the required grade, form, dimension and condition actually be made or processed?
02 · QualificationIs the route acceptable for the buyer, project, origin rule or approved-mill list?
03 · EvidenceWhat MTC, heat traceability, inspection, test or trial proof is required?
04 · CommercialMOQ, lead time, trial quantity, processing and backup route are stated explicitly.
