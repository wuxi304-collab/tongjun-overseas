Technical RFQ
Before submitting

Eight fields prevent most sourcing errors.

  1. Grade / UNS
  2. Product standard
  3. Product form & size
  4. Condition / heat treatment
  5. Quantity
  6. Application
  7. Certificate / inspection
  8. Origin / approved-mill restriction

The form first attempts secure server-side routing and preserves campaign attribution. Until the production mailbox/webhook is configured, it automatically falls back to a structured email to ask2205@outlook.com.

What happens after submission

Your requirement is screened for technical feasibility and qualification boundaries before we recommend a supply route. Grade identity is the first gate; dimensional capability, source role and approval status are verified separately.

What the technical review should return
01 · Route

Manufacturing, approved-origin or stock/processing starting path.

02 · Gaps

Missing specification, size, condition, approval or inspection questions.

03 · Evidence

Documents and verification that must travel with the material.

04 · Commercial basis

MOQ, lead-time, trial/scale and backup-route assumptions.
