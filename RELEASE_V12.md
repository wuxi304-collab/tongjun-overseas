# Tongjun Special Metals — V12

## Release theme
**ATI-inspired industrial enterprise redesign**

V12 does not add more catalogue pages. It strengthens the visual and information architecture across the existing site so Tongjun reads like a serious specialty-materials company rather than a SaaS/AI landing page.

## Design direction
Benchmark reviewed: ATI Materials (`atimaterials.com`) — especially the home page, Specialty Materials, Specialty Rolled Products and All Products architecture, plus ATI's published identity standards.

The implementation is original. No ATI logo, starburst, registered slogan, website source code, proprietary photography or protected branded asset is copied.

## What changed
- Two-tier industrial corporate navigation.
- Original `TJ` monogram treatment for Tongjun; no ATI mark imitation.
- Full-bleed industrial hero with dark image treatment and hard-edged CTA system.
- Structural blue + warm orange action rhythm.
- Helvetica/Arial-like industrial corporate typography.
- Square modules, hard rules and minimal shadow/rounding.
- Product modules shifted from Apple/SaaS cards to image-led materials blocks.
- Market tiles use application photography and stronger labels.
- Capability, route, resource and FAQ blocks converted to enterprise grid language.
- Detail-page heroes converted to blue industrial headers.
- RFQ form converted to engineering/procurement visual language.
- Mobile hero and bottom actions reworked to preserve enterprise character.
- Theme color updated to the V12 structural blue.

## Tongjun differentiation retained
- `TONGJUN SPECIAL METALS`
- `Special Metals. Precisely Sourced.`
- Sourcing + mill qualification + non-standard development positioning.
- No implication that Tongjun owns mills, warehouses or end-user projects it does not own.
- Manufacturing / international-origin / stock-and-processing roles remain distinct.
- MTC / heat / inspection / qualification boundaries remain explicit.
