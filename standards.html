Standards

A grade name is not a complete specification.

International sourcing depends on the product standard, product form, condition, dimensions, testing and end-use qualification. The same alloy designation can lead to different supply routes.

Specification first
Material familyTypical standards to confirmProduct formsWhat still needs to be specified
Stainless sheet / plateASTM A240 / A480 · EN 10088-2 · project-specific ASME/EN requirementsSheet · plate · coilGrade, thickness/width, finish, edge, tolerances, testing, pressure-equipment requirements
Duplex / super duplexASTM A240 / relevant ASME · EN 10088-2Plate · sheet · coilPREN, phase balance, impact/corrosion testing, heat treatment, UT/PMI and project specification
Nickel alloy plate / sheetASTM B443 / B424 / B575 and grade-specific standardsSheet · plate · stripUNS, solution treatment, dimensions, NACE/project requirements, MTC/PMI/inspection
Nickel alloy bar / forgingASTM B446 / B425 and grade/product-specific standards; AMS when aerospace appliesBar · billet · ring · forgingMelt route, grain size, UT class, mechanical testing, aerospace/customer approval
Precision stripASTM / EN grade base plus buyer drawing or internal material specificationStrip · foil · slit coilTemper/tensile, flatness, camber, burr, edge, surface, winding, coil weight and lot consistency
Invar / low expansionCustomer/project/producer specification; ASTM B753 only where its thermostat-component sheet/strip scope is applicableStrip · sheet · plate · bar · forgingCTE window, heat treatment, residual stress, dimensional stability, weldability and application approval
Titanium / zirconiumASTM B265 / B338 / B348 and ASME/project standards as applicablePlate · tube · bar · forgingGrade, product standard, heat treatment, surface, NDT, pressure-equipment and origin requirements
Standards hygiene: ASTM F1684 was withdrawn in 2024 with no replacement and is not used here as a current default procurement path. ASTM B753 is active but scoped to thermostat component alloys, so it is only referenced where applicable. For LNG, tooling and other 36Ni-Fe uses, confirm the current buyer/project/producer specification and CTE acceptance method.

Important: the standards above are a sourcing map, not a substitute for the buyer's purchase specification. Final supply is always checked against the complete order requirement.
