# Week 1 Outbound Rules

## Gate before send

1. Confirm company role: end user / distributor / processor / mill / agent.
2. Confirm the target contact is procurement, sourcing, product management, engineering or quality — not a generic marketing contact unless no alternative exists.
3. Verify the recipient email.
4. Send only from `ask2205@outlook.com` or the later branded mailbox.
5. Keep `ac` stable for the account across Touch 1–4.
6. Do not put buyer name, personal email, project name, drawing number or quotation data in the URL.
7. CTA is one current “problem order”, not a catalogue request.
8. State qualification boundaries explicitly. Do not imply approved source, stock or mill status without evidence.

## Sequence

- Touch 1: account-specific sourcing-gap hypothesis.
- Touch 2: three business days later; ask for one difficult requirement.
- Touch 3: eight business days after Touch 1; give one technical route insight.
- Touch 4: eighteen business days after Touch 1; close the loop and keep the China route open for future gaps.
- Stop after Touch 4 unless they engage.
