Invar 36 for LNG membrane supply chains.

Generic 4J36 availability is not the same as a membrane-qualified supply route. LNG programs require dimensional consistency, low-expansion behavior, weldability, continuous strip quality and project-specific approval.

Application-specific sourcing
Target requirement

Treat 0.70 × 500 mm as a qualification target, not a catalogue size.

Dimensions such as 0.70 × 500 mm must be confirmed against actual mill capability, continuous length, flatness and qualification route.

01

CTE control

Chemical composition and heat treatment must support the required low-expansion behavior.

02

Flatness

Thin strip must remain stable for downstream membrane fabrication and welding.

03

Continuous length

Coil length, weld/splice policy and lot consistency are qualification variables.

04

Weldability

Material behavior must be validated against the fabrication process used by the membrane supply chain.

05

Traceability

Heat number, MTC, process route and inspection records require full traceability.

06

Approval boundary

GTT, shipyard or approved-vendor status is separate from generic 4J36 manufacturing capability.

Supply route

Use a dual-path qualification strategy.

We typically separate the route that can satisfy immediate qualification from the route intended for later China-origin cost and scale advantages.

01

Qualification route

International-origin or already accepted route where project requirements demand it.

02

China development route

Domestic 4J36 route assessed for exact dimensions, CTE, flatness, length and consistency.

03

Processor route

Slitting or finishing only after upstream heat and strip capability are confirmed.

04

Evidence gate

No supply claim is treated as qualified until documents, sample material and application approval align.
