Product forms
Precision Strip & Foil

Control the window, not just the nominal size.

Typical enquiry fields include grade, standard, thickness, width, temper or strength window, edge condition, flatness, camber, burr, surface, ID/OD, coil weight and traceability.

301 / 304 / 316L17-7PH / AM350A286625 / 718 / C276 / X-750
±?tolerance
We do not publish a universal tolerance before confirming the exact mill/processor route. Precision capability must be verified by grade, thickness, width and temper.
Sheet & Plate

From standard mill sizes to project cut dimensions.

Nickel alloys, stainless, duplex and low-expansion materials can move through different manufacturing and stock routes depending on required origin and certificate.

S

Sheet

Thin gauge, controlled surface and flatness for forming and fabricated components.

P

Plate

Project dimensions, cut sizes and inspection package matched to equipment requirements.

Q

Qualification Plate

Small lots or named-origin material used to support early technical approval before scale-up.

Heavy & Extra-wide Plate

Large dimensions change the supply chain.

Width, thickness, heat treatment capacity, UT requirements, flatness, rolling route and equipment-code documentation can eliminate otherwise capable mills.

  • Extra-wide stainless plate
  • Duplex / super duplex project plate
  • Nickel alloy heavy plate — route verified by actual product form
  • Titanium plate for process equipment
  • PMI / UT / third-party inspection coordination
