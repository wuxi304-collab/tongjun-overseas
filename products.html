PRODUCT FORMS

Product form is the real sourcing language.

Global buyers rarely procure “an alloy” in the abstract. They need a qualified combination of grade, form, size, condition, tolerances, surface, documentation and approval status.

Start with form

For non-standard requirements, geometry and processing route can be as important as alloy chemistry.

HOW WE DEFINE A PRODUCT

Grade + form + condition + evidence.

That combination is the minimum meaningful sourcing definition for special metals.

01

Grade

UNS / EN / JIS / GB identity, chemistry boundaries and substitution rules.

02

Form

Strip, sheet, plate, bar, forging, tube or powder.

03

Condition

Annealed, solution treated, aged, cold worked, temper or surface condition.

04

Evidence

MTC, PMI, NDT, dimensional records, process certificates and project approvals.
