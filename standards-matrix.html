Revision control

Base specification numbers are shown without assuming that the website carries the newest revision. The purchasing document must name the required revision or state how revision selection is controlled.

Five checks

Before a standard becomes a purchase requirement.

Specification number alone is incomplete without product form, revision, condition and supplementary requirements.

01Scope

Does the document actually cover the requested alloy and product form?

02Revision

Current revision, purchaser-named revision or contractual legacy revision?

03Condition

Annealed, solution treated, aged, cold worked or project-defined?

04Supplementary requirements

NDT, corrosion test, grain size, impact, PMI, heat treatment, marking and traceability.
