03 / Low Expansion

Invar 36 / 4J36 is an application-specific supply chain.

LNG membrane strip and aerospace tooling plate use the same alloy family but demand entirely different dimensions, thermal stability, flatness, stress state and qualification routes.

LNG target: 0.70 × 500 mm Invar 36 is treated as a dedicated development and qualification path, not a generic 4J36 strip enquiry.

Two distinct routes

  • LNG membrane: thin strip, continuous length, flatness, weldability, CTE and containment-system qualification.
  • Aerospace tooling: plate thickness, residual stress, CTE curve, UT, welding distortion and machining stability.
