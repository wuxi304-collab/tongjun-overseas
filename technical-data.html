Document hierarchy

What controls the order?

A generic data sheet is useful for orientation. It sits below the buyer's actual technical and contractual requirements.

01Purchase order / customer specification

Grade, form, size, condition, inspection, approval and acceptance criteria.

02Applicable current product standard

ASTM, ASME, AMS, EN or project-specific requirements for that product form.

03Approved source + mill documentation

MTC, heat identity, process route, NDT, test reports and source approvals.

04Reference technical data

Useful for material orientation — never the sole purchase or design basis.

Data governance

Technical content has a status.

  1. Every exact value is tagged as reference, nominal, typical or specification-linked.
  2. Product-form standards are shown separately rather than mixing plate, bar, forging and tube requirements.
  3. Withdrawn or obsolete standards are not used as default procurement paths.
  4. Final supply evidence comes from the certified source and the purchase specification — not from this website.
