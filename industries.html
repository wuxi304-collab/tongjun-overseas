Aerospace & Tooling

High-temperature alloys, titanium and low-expansion tooling materials.

Typical routes include Alloy 718, A286, X-750, Invar 36 tooling plate and titanium bar/forging. Final supply depends on the customer material specification and approval path.

  • Composite tooling and Invar structures
  • High-temperature bar / forging
  • Master alloy and cast-component supply chains
  • Titanium aerospace materials
LNG & Cryogenic

Low-temperature supply requires system-level qualification.

Invar 36 membrane strip and 304L cryogenic sheet are treated as dedicated application routes. Dimensional match alone is not sufficient.

Qualification principle: material chemistry → product form → process stability → welding/forming behaviour → containment/shipyard approval.
Bellows & Precision Components

Thin-gauge performance under cyclic service.

Relevant materials include 316L, 625, C276, AM350, 17-7PH and related grades. Thin strip needs a controlled mechanical and edge-condition window for forming and fatigue performance.

Buyer concernSourcing response
Burr / edge crackDefine edge process and measurable acceptance.
Flatness / camberConfirm grade-thickness-width route before quotation.
Strength windowSpecify tensile / hardness condition, not vague temper labels.
ConsistencyControl heat, coil and processing traceability.
Chemical & Process Equipment

Corrosion alloys and reactive metals for severe service.

625, 825, C276, C22, 904L, 254SMO, duplex, titanium and zirconium can be combined into project supply packages when qualification and origin requirements are clear.

  • Reactors and pressure vessels
  • Columns, drums and heat exchangers
  • Welded plate heat exchangers
  • Clad / corrosion-resistant material packages
