Screening, not substitution

Compare the decision variables that actually change the route.

Density is easy to compare. Service chemistry, product form, heat treatment, code basis, dimensional capability and qualification evidence are harder — and usually more important.

BoundaryNo comparison result on this page authorizes a substitution. Final selection must be checked against service conditions, current governing specification, customer approval and certified source documentation.
Decision sequence

Use the comparison in the right order.

Start with failure mechanism and service environment, then product form and qualification. Price and lead time come after technical fit.

01Service condition

Corrosion chemistry, temperature, load, dimensional stability and failure mode.

02Material family

Stainless, duplex, nickel, low-expansion or titanium route.

03Product form

Strip, sheet, plate, bar, forging, pipe/tube or engineered component.

04Qualification

Standard, source approval, MTC, NDT, testing and customer acceptance.
