# Release V24 — Launch Readiness & Corporate Editorial Polish

Date: 2026-09-07

V24 is a consolidation release. It adds no new sourcing workflow. Instead it reduces duplicate decision content, differentiates the major material landing pages, tightens mobile navigation and removes public-facing wording that reads like internal scaffolding.

## Changes

- Homepage: four overlapping evidence / buyer / offer / tools blocks are consolidated into one Buyer Decision Spine.
- Homepage RFQ: duplicate short form is replaced by a direct handoff to the full controlled Technical RFQ.
- Quality: redundant generic qualification cards are removed; the page begins with Claim → Record → Link → Release and moves directly into source roles, custody, evidence and buyer acceptance.
- Material landings: Precision Strip, Nickel Alloys, Invar 36, Heavy Plate and Titanium + Zirconium now expose different primary qualification variables instead of sharing a generic buyer checklist.
- Flagship technical references: a compact purchase-focus rail differentiates the procurement problem for 625, 718, C-276 and 36Ni-Fe without changing controlled technical values or revision numbers.
- Mobile navigation: resources are grouped into Buyer Tools, quick technical entry points are promoted, only one accordion stays open, and the menu closes cleanly on navigation / Escape.
- Corporate editorial audit: unfinished mail wording and defensive public copy were removed or rewritten as operating rules.

## Technical-data boundary

No chemistry, mechanical-property, density or standard-route value is changed by V24. Controlled flagship technical references therefore remain at their existing revision state.
