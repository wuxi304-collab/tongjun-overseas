What matters

Define the CTE acceptance before the source route.

Invar-type supply is application-specific. Material identity, thermal-expansion requirement, test range, product form and condition must be fixed before generic availability becomes useful.

01

CTE control

Coefficient of thermal expansion is a primary functional requirement and should be reviewed by temperature range and batch.

02

Thin-strip route

LNG-type thin strip requires dimensional consistency, flatness, weldability and continuous-length control.

03

Tooling plate route

Aerospace tooling plate requires residual-stress control, flatness, UT and dimensional stability after machining/welding.

04

Heat treatment

Condition and stress-relief route can materially affect dimensional performance.

05

Batch consistency

A nominal 36% Ni chemistry is not enough; application validation depends on repeatable physical performance.

06

Qualification boundary

A supplier able to make 4J36 is not automatically qualified for LNG membrane or aerospace tooling applications.
