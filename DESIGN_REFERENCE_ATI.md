# DESIGN REFERENCE — ATI-inspired industrial enterprise system

## Intent
V13 uses ATI Materials as the principal *design-language and information-architecture* benchmark for Tongjun Special Metals. The goal is to look and behave like a mature global specialty-materials company while keeping Tongjun's actual operating model explicit: sourcing, qualification and non-standard development rather than mill ownership.

## Current ATI patterns reviewed on 2026-09-06
- Corporate navigation organized around Businesses + Products, Markets, About and buyer resources.
- Specialty business pages repeat a strong local section navigation: About, Products + Materials, Manufacturing Capabilities, Operations, Tools + Resources.
- Product architecture separates material families from product forms.
- Market pages lead from difficult service conditions to material capability.
- Tools + Resources are treated as a first-class corporate section, not blog content.
- Specialty Rolled Products describes coil, strip, foil and plate separately and emphasizes process capability.
- Specialty Materials separates Materials, Products and Capabilities into distinct buyer entry points.

## What V13 intentionally borrows
- Industrial enterprise visual hierarchy instead of SaaS/card language.
- Structural blue with restrained orange action accents.
- Corporate grotesk typography and hard edges.
- Large industrial photography and technical product categorization.
- Mega navigation for Materials + Products and Markets.
- Secondary section navigation on category pages.
- Multiple portfolio entry points: Material / Product Form / Market.
- Product finder / technical resource behavior.
- Application-first editorial language.

## Tongjun-specific differentiation
- Brand: TONGJUN SPECIAL METALS.
- Promise: `Special Metals. Precisely Sourced.`
- Operating model: Special Metals Sourcing, Mill Qualification & Non-standard Material Development from China.
- Public site never implies Tongjun owns the mills or facilities shown conceptually.
- Manufacturing route, approved-origin route and stock/processing route remain separate.
- Exact grade/form/size/condition/qualification status is confirmed per RFQ.

- `/alloys` — searchable 28-alloy technical index.

## Guardrail
“ATI-like” means information architecture, visual discipline, industrial photography ratio, section rhythm and technical buyer utility. It does **not** mean copying ATI's trade dress, logo/starburst, proprietary photography, registered slogans, source code, wording or customer references.
