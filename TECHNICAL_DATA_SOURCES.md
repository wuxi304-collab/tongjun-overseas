## ASTM standards hygiene for 36Ni-Fe
- ASTM F1684: withdrawn 2024 with no replacement; historical scope included conventional 36Ni-Fe UNS K93603.
- ASTM B753: active thermostat-component-alloy specification; not treated as a universal procurement standard for LNG, tooling, plate/bar or every low-expansion application.
- Public default: buyer/project/producer specification + explicit CTE acceptance criteria; B753 only where applicable.

## V19 source verification — 2026-09-07
- Special Metals current Technical Bulletin index confirms producer references for INCONEL 625 (UNS N06625), INCONEL 718 (UNS N07718), INCONEL C-276 (UNS N10276), and NILO Alloy 36 family references.
- Special Metals NILO/NILOMAG bulletin reports NILO Alloy 36 nominal 36Ni/64Fe, density 8.11 g/cm3 and low-expansion reference data.
- Haynes current C-276 literature confirms form-specific routes including B575 flat product, B574 bar, B622 seamless pipe/tube, B619/B626 welded pipe/tube and B564 forgings.
- Carpenter current Invar 36 page lists UNS K93601/K93603 and ASTM B753 Alloy T36 as an applicable specification for its product route.
- ASTM F1684-06(2021) remains officially withdrawn in 2024 with no replacement listed.

## Publishing rule
Public pages must distinguish reference/typical/nominal data from specification minima/maxima. Final procurement is governed by the PO, current applicable standard, buyer/project specification, approved source controls and MTC/test reports. The source register supports material identity and product-form routing; it is not a blanket supply claim.
