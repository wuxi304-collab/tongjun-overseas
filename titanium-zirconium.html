Titanium & zirconium
What matters

Reactive-metal quality depends on the entire processing boundary.

For titanium and zirconium, grade identity must stay connected to the product-form standard, dimensions, surface condition, processing controls, inspection scope and traceability.

01

Grade & product standard

ASTM / ASME product form, chemistry, condition and testing are checked together.

02

Plate / tube matching

Project packages often require coordinated plate, tube, bar or forging routes rather than one isolated item.

03

Surface & cleanliness

Handling and contamination control matter for reactive metals and downstream fabrication.

04

Inspection

PMI, dimensional inspection, NDT and third-party inspection are coordinated by project.

05

Fabrication route

Formability, welding procedure and equipment fabrication requirements are considered during sourcing.

06

Origin & qualification

Mill and qualification restrictions are captured before the commercial route is selected.
