# RELEASE V18 — Source-Role & Chain-of-Custody System

Date: 2026-09-07

## Objective

V18 makes source responsibility explicit across the public procurement story. The site now separates who manufactures the material, who holds custody, who changes product form, and who coordinates the qualified supply route.

## Quality + Traceability

- Reframed the page around **evidence that travels with the material**.
- Added a four-role Source-Role Ledger: Manufacturer / Stockholder / Processor / Tongjun Supply Desk.
- Added a six-stage chain-of-custody map: Source → Heat/Lot → Standard → Processing → Inspection → Delivery.
- Replaced defensive public language with an explicit qualification-boundary model.

## Cross-site tone cleanup

- Capabilities now presents the three-source model as resilience architecture rather than a rebuttal.
- Distribution Supply Desk is framed as an extension for constrained routes while preserving buyer approval.
- RFQ and Problem Order language now uses positive qualification gates instead of defensive disclaimers.

## Boundary

Source-role clarity does not change legal manufacturing origin. Secondary processing remains a separate documented responsibility layer. Buyer/OEM/project approval remains independent of generic manufacturing capability.
