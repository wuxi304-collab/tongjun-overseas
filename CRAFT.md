# CRAFT.md — ExoticAlloyCN design discipline

## Character
Industrial, technical, restrained, global. The site should feel closer to an advanced-materials engineering organization than to a commodity trading marketplace.

## Layout
- Full-bleed photography for major story beats.
- Square cards and modules; avoid SaaS-style rounded containers.
- Strong section rules, technical labels and tabular evidence.
- Large narrow-grotesk headings; compact uppercase microcopy.
- Use whitespace to signal authority, not decoration.

## Color
- Graphite / deep navy as structural backgrounds.
- Industrial blue as primary system color.
- Orange only for priority, route, state or CTA emphasis.

## Copy
- Lead with application, environment, product form and qualification.
- Prefer `qualified route`, `source role`, `evidence package`, `trial-to-scale`, `product-form standard`.
- Avoid generic claims such as best quality, world class, leading supplier, direct mill, always in stock.

## Image rule
Every image should plausibly exist inside a real special-metals mill, service center, inspection lab or end-use engineering environment. Avoid glossy CGI, floating metal objects, impossible reflections and generic AI factory scenes.
