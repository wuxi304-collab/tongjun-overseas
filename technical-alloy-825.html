Technical overview

Material identity is only the first gate.

A titanium-stabilized nickel-iron-chromium alloy with molybdenum and copper, used where resistance to acids, chloride stress-corrosion and localized corrosion must be balanced with fabrication and pressure-equipment requirements.

01Sulfuric / phosphoric acid equipment
02Oil and gas process components
03Heat exchangers and pickling systems
04Pollution-control and chemical-processing equipment

Sourcing qualification

What must be proved before supply.

  1. Specify whether stabilization/heat-treatment requirements are mandatory.
  2. Corrosion performance must be checked against actual process chemistry and temperature.
  3. For plate, separate material standard from pressure-equipment code and NDE requirements.
  4. For urgent small lots, compare international-origin stock routes against China production rather than forcing one route.
