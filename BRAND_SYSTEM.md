# Brand & Visual System V1

## Market brand
**TONGJUN SPECIAL METALS**

## Positioning
Special Metals Sourcing, Mill Qualification & Non-standard Material Development from China.

## Hero line
**Special Metals. Precisely Sourced.**

## Visual principles
- Industrial editorial, not “factory template” design.
- White / graphite / silver / industrial blue.
- Use engineering data, dimensions and process diagrams as visual assets.
- No supplier/customer logo wall without permission.
- No unlicensed photos from international competitors.
- AI-generated visuals may be used as clearly generic conceptual brand imagery, never as fake case studies or fake factory evidence.
- Prioritize owned photography of coil, strip, plate, labels, heat numbers, processing, packaging and inspection over generic stock imagery.

## Tone
Precise, technical, restrained, commercially useful. Avoid “world-leading”, “best quality”, “one-stop solution” and unsupported scale claims.
