Precision strip & foil
What matters

Repeatability is the product.

Nominal thickness alone does not qualify precision strip. Rolling, slitting, edge finishing, mechanical condition and final coil geometry must stay inside the same application window.

01

Thickness & width

The nominal size is only the starting point. We verify achievable tolerances by alloy, condition and rolling route.

02

Temper / strength window

Hardness, tensile strength and spring properties are treated as controlled purchase requirements, not catalogue labels.

03

Edge condition

Deburred, rounded, slit or special edge requirements are mapped to the processor and final application.

04

Flatness & camber

Critical for stamping, bellows, springs and precision formed components.

05

Coil consistency

Coil weight, winding, splice policy and lot-to-lot stability are part of qualification.

06

Traceability

Heat/lot identification, MTC and inspection records are retained through processing routes.
