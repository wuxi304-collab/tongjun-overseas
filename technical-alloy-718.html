Sourcing qualification

What must be proved before supply.

  1. Do not quote 718 without the exact heat-treatment / metallurgical condition.
  2. Aerospace requirements can specify VIM + VAR/ESR, approved source, grain size and NDT.
  3. Sheet/strip availability does not imply precision-gauge capability at the requested width.
  4. Mechanical properties must be tied to form, section size, condition and governing AMS/ASTM revision.
