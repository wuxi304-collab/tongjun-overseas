Procurement evidence map

Build the acceptance file before the shipment.

A technical data sheet describes the material. A procurement evidence map describes what must accompany the material for this specific route to become auditable and acceptable.

01Identity anchor

UNS N06625 plus the product-form specification appropriate to sheet/plate, bar, forging or tube.

02Condition + route

Ordered condition and heat treatment are confirmed; melt or source-control requirements are added when the customer or code requires them.

03Processing traceability

Cutting, slitting, machining or other secondary work retains the link to heat identity and the declared manufacturing source.

MTC is the core record; dimensions, PMI, UT/NDE, TPI and ASME/NACE/OEM/project approvals are added only where the PO requires them.

Sourcing qualification

What must be proved before supply.

  1. Confirm annealed / solution-treated condition and any heat-treatment requirement.
  2. Treat precision strip width × thickness as a separate manufacturability gate.
  3. For pressure or sour-service use, confirm ASME/NACE/ISO/project requirements independently.
  4. For aerospace, mill approval, melt route, AMS condition and customer source control can override generic ASTM availability.
