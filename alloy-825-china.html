Before quotation

Eight fields define the real requirement.

A disciplined RFQ avoids the false precision of quoting a grade without knowing the product form, qualification path or origin restriction.

1 · Grade / UNSCommercial and equivalent designation.
2 · StandardASTM / ASME / AMS / EN / customer spec.
3 · Product form & sizeThickness, width, length, diameter, coil data.
4 · ConditionHeat treatment, temper, surface, edge.
5 · QuantityTrial quantity vs commercial scale.
6 · ApplicationWhat the material will actually do.
7 · InspectionMTC, PMI, UT, corrosion or witness needs.
8 · OriginOpen, China acceptable, or named mill.

FAQ

What buyers usually need to clarify.

The commercial answer improves as the technical boundary becomes more explicit.

Can you quote this material directly from the page?

The page is a sourcing and qualification entry point. A commercial quote requires grade/UNS, standard, product form, size, condition, quantity, application, certificate and origin requirements.

Is China origin always the primary route?

No. Chinese production, international-origin and stock/processing routes are compared against qualification, MOQ, lead time, cost and named-mill restrictions.

Do listed product forms mean every dimension is available?

No. Product form and dimensional capability are confirmed against the exact RFQ. Difficult thickness/width combinations are treated as separate feasibility checks.

Can third-party inspection be coordinated?

Where required, inspection needs such as PMI, UT, dimensional checks or third-party witnessing can be included in the sourcing plan, subject to the governing specification.
